    Questions people ask

    How do I place an order?

    Add items to your cart, fill in your details, and tap Send order on WhatsApp. We reply to confirm the total and delivery time.

    Can I buy on credit?

    No. Every order is paid by EFT or cash. This keeps our prices low for everyone.

    How fast is delivery?

    Standard orders are delivered on the next delivery day. Choose rush at checkout if you need it the same day.

    Do you do big orders for events?

    Yes. Use the bulk quote form above for 5L juice, re-seller stock, and combo packs.

    What if an item is out of stock?

    We will tell you on WhatsApp before you pay and offer a similar item or a refund.
