How to order

  1. Choose your items in the Shop tab and add them to your cart.
  2. Enter your name and delivery area in the cart.
  3. Place your order. You get an order number straight away.
  4. Track it in the Track order tab. We confirm delivery time and payment with you by phone.

Delivery & rush orders

We deliver locally. Rush orders carry an extra fee. Bulk and re-seller orders: message us for special pricing.

Who we are

Nollie's is a small business selling juice, perfume, combo packs, hampers and cereals. We are responsible for the personal information you give us on this website.

What we collect

Your name, phone number, delivery area, any note you add, and the items you order. We do not ask for card or bank details on this website.

Why we collect it

Only to process your order, deliver it, contact you about it, and keep basic sales records for our business.

Who sees it

Only Nollie's, and anyone who helps us deliver your order. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep order records only as long as we need them to run the business and meet our legal obligations. After that we delete them.

Keeping it safe

Orders are stored in a protected database that only we can open. Your cart and last-used details are also saved on your own phone so you do not have to retype them. You can clear them in your browser settings.
